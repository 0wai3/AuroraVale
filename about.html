About AuroraVale

A global platform for international experiences.

AuroraVale is built around a simple idea: international opportunities should connect people and organizations across borders.

Our purpose

Connecting people with the possibility of going further.

AuroraVale provides an online pathway for people and organizations interested in participating in international programs and travel opportunities.

The platform is designed to operate internationally rather than being tied to one country. Participants may apply from different parts of the world and select from destinations made available through active programs.

◎

Global by design

The concept is based on cross-border participation: one country can be the participant's home while another becomes the destination.

Our principles

Simple, transparent participation.

01

Accessibility

The application process is designed to request practical information without unnecessary complexity.

02

International connection

AuroraVale focuses on connecting participants with destinations and experiences outside their home country.

03

Clear participation terms

Program requirements, fees, selection rules and sponsorship terms should be understood before participation.

AuroraVale should only publish program benefits, destinations, selection methods and sponsorship commitments that are actually available under the relevant program terms.
