Application

Applying to participate

Complete the online application with the information requested, select an available program or destination where applicable, and follow the payment and verification steps.
The current application/participation fee is $20, subject to the applicable program terms.
The $20 payment completes the applicable participation/application process. It does not by itself guarantee selection, travel, visa approval or a particular destination.
Once the required application and payment steps have been successfully completed, the applicant can enter the applicable participant process, subject to the program's terms.
Selection

How participants are selected

No. Payment allows participation in the applicable program process but does not guarantee selection or travel.
The selection method depends on the specific program. The applicable program terms should state whether selection uses a draw, review process, eligibility criteria or another defined method.
Selection depends on the capacity and rules of each program. A completed application should therefore be treated as an opportunity to participate in the selection process rather than a guaranteed trip.
Travel

What happens if I am selected?

Where a program provides full travel sponsorship, applicable benefits may include international airfare, accommodation, meals or living support, local transportation and other eligible program-related travel expenses.
Visa costs may be covered where the selected program explicitly provides for them. Visa approval itself is always subject to the relevant immigration authority.
Participants are responsible for obtaining and maintaining their own valid passport unless a particular program explicitly states otherwise.
No. AuroraVale cannot guarantee an immigration authority's decision. Participants must satisfy the destination country's visa and entry requirements.
