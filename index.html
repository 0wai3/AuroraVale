How the opportunity works

From your country to another.

A participant in Pakistan may choose Australia. A participant in Australia may choose Japan. Someone in India may choose the United Kingdom. AuroraVale is designed around cross-border participation.

  • Applications accepted internationally
  • Multiple destinations can be offered
  • Participants can identify a preferred destination
  • Completed applications enter the applicable selection process
  • Selected participants may receive sponsored travel support

What happens after applying?

A straightforward participation journey.

1

Submit your application

Provide the basic information required for your selected program.

2

Complete participation payment

The current program participation/application fee is $20, subject to the applicable program terms.

3

Become a participant

After successful completion of the applicable process, your application enters the participant pool.

4

Selection

Participants may be selected according to the rules of the applicable program.

5

Travel preparation

Selected participants receive destination-specific instructions and preparation requirements.

6

International experience

Where the program provides sponsorship, eligible travel and living expenses are covered according to the applicable terms.

Important: Paying the $20 participation/application fee does not by itself guarantee selection, travel, visa approval, or a particular destination. Sponsored benefits apply only to participants selected under the applicable program terms.
