Travel readiness

Organizations & representatives

Participation doesn't need to be unnecessarily complicated.

Where the relevant program permits organization representation, applicants can provide their organization or initiative information and identify themselves as a representative.

The exact documentation requirements depend on the program. AuroraVale should only request documentation that is genuinely necessary for participation, verification, travel or compliance.

Information that may be requested

  • Full name
  • Country and city
  • Contact information
  • Organization or initiative name, where applicable
  • Preferred destination
  • Passport availability confirmation
  • Basic reason for participation

Having an organization or representative profile does not by itself guarantee selection. Likewise, completing the application and $20 participation fee does not guarantee international travel.
