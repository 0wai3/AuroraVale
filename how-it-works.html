How It Works

A simple journey from application to possibility.

AuroraVale is structured around a straightforward participation process: apply, complete the participation fee, enter the participant pool, and proceed through the applicable selection process.

1

Complete the application

Enter the basic personal, organization and destination information requested.

2

Complete the $20 payment

The current participation/application fee is $20 under the stated program structure.

3

Become a participant

Once the applicable application and payment requirements are completed, the applicant becomes a participant in the relevant program process.

4

Selection

Participants may be selected according to the rules established for the specific program.

5

Preparation

Selected participants receive additional instructions, documentation requirements and travel information.

6

Sponsored travel

Where sponsorship applies, eligible transportation, accommodation and living support are provided according to the program terms.

01

Apply internationally

Applicants can come from different countries and regions.

02

Select a destination

Choose from destinations made available through the relevant program.

03

Await selection

Participation creates an opportunity to be selected but does not itself guarantee selection.

The $20 payment is a participation/application fee. It should not be described as a payment that guarantees a trip, selection, visa, flight or destination. Sponsored travel applies only where the participant is selected and the applicable program provides those benefits.
