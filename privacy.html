Privacy

Privacy Policy

How AuroraVale may handle information submitted through the website.

This Privacy Policy describes the general approach to information submitted through AuroraVale websites and application systems. The final policy should be reviewed and updated before launch to reflect the actual legal entity, hosting providers, payment processor, email provider and applicable laws.

Information we may collect

Depending on the application process, AuroraVale may collect information such as name, country, city, email address, telephone number, organization details, destination preference and other information necessary for participation.

How information may be used

Information may be used to process applications, communicate with applicants, verify participation information, administer programs, arrange travel for selected participants and provide customer support.

Payment information

Payment information should be processed through a secure payment provider. AuroraVale should not store complete payment card details unless it has a legitimate and compliant system specifically designed for that purpose.

Travel information

Selected participants may need to provide additional information necessary for travel arrangements. The website should only request information that is reasonably necessary for the applicable travel process.

Information security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures should be used to protect participant information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure or loss.

Third-party services

AuroraVale may use third-party services for hosting, payment processing, email, analytics, verification and travel-related services. Their use should be disclosed in the final production privacy policy where applicable.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact AuroraVale through the official contact information published on the live website.
