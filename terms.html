The general terms governing participation in AuroraVale programs.

1. Participation

By submitting an application, the applicant agrees to provide accurate information and comply with the applicable program terms.

2. Participation Fee

The current participation/application fee is $20 unless a specific program states otherwise. Payment of the fee does not itself guarantee selection, travel, visa approval or a specific destination.

3. Selection

Selection is conducted according to the process applicable to the relevant program. The selection method and available number of participants may vary.

4. Sponsored Travel

Where a selected program provides sponsored travel, the specific benefits will be defined by that program. These may include airfare, accommodation, eligible living expenses, local transportation and eligible visa costs.

5. Passport and Immigration

Participants are responsible for having valid travel documentation. Visa applications remain subject to the decisions of the relevant immigration authorities.

6. Destination Availability

Destinations displayed on the website may change. A destination being shown on the website does not necessarily mean that applications for that destination remain open at all times.

7. Accurate Information

False, misleading or materially incomplete information may result in an application being rejected or participation being cancelled according to the applicable program rules.

8. Changes to Programs

Program dates, destinations, selection processes and travel arrangements may change where circumstances require. Final arrangements should be confirmed through the official program communication provided to selected participants.

9. Final Program Terms

Where a specific program has additional terms, those terms may supplement these general website terms.

10. Contact

Questions regarding these terms should be directed through the official AuroraVale contact channels published on the live website.
