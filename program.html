Global Programs

International opportunities without one fixed destination.

AuroraVale programs are designed around international participation. Apply from your home country, select an available destination and participate in the applicable selection process.

The concept

Your home country doesn't have to be your destination.

The defining feature of AuroraVale is cross-border participation. A participant may apply from one country and express interest in a program located in another.

Example

A participant based in Pakistan could apply for an available Australia program. A participant based in Australia could apply for an available European or Asian destination.

Program structure

What participation can include.

✈

Cross-Cultural Experience

Representation

Where applicable, participants may travel as representatives of an organization, initiative or participating group.

Potential sponsored support

Selected participants may receive comprehensive travel support.

Where a program specifically provides sponsorship, the applicable terms can include travel and living support.

01 International airfare
02 Accommodation
03 Living or meal support
04 Local transportation

Visa costs may be included only where the specific program terms state that they are covered. Passport issuance and personal documentation remain the participant's responsibility unless otherwise stated.
